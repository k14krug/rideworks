# P4-02 — Training State: product requirements and design decisions

**Status:** Approved core calculation model, data-source principles and [screen design](P4-02-training-state-screen.md); implementation underway in [draft PR #24](https://github.com/k14krug/rideworks/pull/24). **HARD — Analyst Gate 1 remains open.**
**Decision context:** P4-01 accepted evidence policy; P4-02 design decisions dated 2026-10-09; source-eligibility clarification informed by the 2026-10-10 running-app review. [Current JIT](../tasks/P4-02.md).

## 1. Product goal and acceptance standard

RideWorks should provide a **credible, interpretable account of the rider's training history and current modeled training load**. It should recognize the contribution of substantial training sessions using the best defensible information available, including approximate HR-based load where qualified measured power is absent. Fitness, Fatigue and Form should reflect changes in recent versus accumulated training effort, rather than primarily reflecting gaps in detailed source recordings.

Interpretability and longitudinal consistency matter more than laboratory-level precision. Fitness/Fatigue/Form are **model outputs**, not measured physiological fitness, fatigue, health or readiness. Source limitations should be visible where relevant without overwhelming ordinary use.

**Primary behavioral and method reference (confirmed 2026-10-10):** Elevate's Fitness Trend is the **default design reference** for RideWorks' training-stress source preference and longitudinal behavior, especially power-based stress in virtual workouts and HR-based stress outdoors. **Follow the reference method unless there is a specific, documented, compelling evidence-based reason to depart from it.** The referenced implementation calculates cycling PSS using available wattage/time streams for weighted power plus reported moving time and FTP, and selects available **power-meter PSS ahead of HRSS** for Fitness Trend. An exhaustive, gap-free one-Hz file and best-20-minute Performance eligibility are not general prerequisites in Elevate. See [Elevate's activity computer](https://github.com/thomaschampagne/elevate/blob/develop/appcore/modules/shared/sync/compute/activity-computer.ts) and [Fitness Trend selection](https://github.com/thomaschampagne/elevate/blob/develop/appcore/src/app/fitness-trend/shared/services/fitness.service.ts). **Do not assume the reference correctly reconstructs every missing second**: available-stream weighted power and reported-duration pairing are approximate when samples are absent or pauses uncertain. RideWorks should explicitly label such session-level PSS as an estimate, retain untouched raw measurements and report evidence gaps. Exact score parity is not required; the approved dated FTP and stronger known source evidence may legitimately change the result. Each **material** deviation from Elevate's behavior must identify the concrete source/accuracy risk and its effect on training trends, not be justified by general conservatism.

**Acceptance checks:** Inspect representative high-load, ordinary and low-load days across recent windows. Where a ride has eligible power or suitable recorded HR/duration, it must contribute its independently calculated stress and produce the expected daily Fitness/Fatigue update and next-day Form effect. Do not silently suppress a material ride solely because a detailed HR stream fails a completeness check if a defensible *independently eligible* same-source HR summary is available. Require real-rider date-level behavioral checks **in addition to** unit arithmetic tests; compare against the external reference as a plausibility check, not as an oracle.

## Power stress strategy — selected product direction, implementation method pending

For a ride with **credible measured power-meter evidence**, evaluate an **Elevate-style session-level power stress estimate** from the available wattage/time data and a compatible reported moving/active duration; prefer that estimate to average-HR-based stress when coverage is reasonably representative. Missing samples change the **evidence quality label and potential uncertainty**; they must not automatically force HR priority or cause the available power effort to disappear. Never promote an incompletely observed stream to a verified fully measured workout; maintain distinct P4-01 **observed interval / FIT gap-corrected / partial** outputs and observed-work kJ alongside any **new model-only estimated whole-session stress**.

**Evidence exceptions:** Verified absence of measured watts, an untrusted/estimated power source, material conflict between source duration and sample timing, very sparse/unrepresentative recorded segments, unverified pauses creating implausible duration, missing dated FTP or conflicting candidate sources can justify rejecting a session-level power estimate. Document the specific reason and choose supported HRSS or unavailable stress accordingly. Respect P4-01's strict rules whenever declaring recorded interval stress complete or corrected, and never interpolate unmeasured **API power** under those rules; the reference-inspired *session estimate* is a distinct derived quantity, not an assertion that API gaps were filled. No change to Performance-v2, canonical FTP, preserved originals, or outdoor Strava estimated-watts exclusion.

**Implementation approval boundary:** The owner selected this **behavioral/method preference**, superseding the earlier automatic HR-before-partial-power order for credible power-meter recordings. The exact representativeness test, normalized-power time treatment, supported moving duration, new score/method label, impact on incomplete sources, and regression evidence must be defined and reviewed at HARD — Analyst before adopting a new production calculation. A single known ride or choosing the larger number is not a general source policy. Test 2026-10-07, other independently selected interval workouts, 2026-09-16 outdoor HR, and contrasting complete/sparse/paused files to demonstrate improvement without hidden overestimation.

## Interval-workout regression — source eligibility clarification, 2026-10-10

**Acceptance principle:** Eligibility for **training-load power stress** is distinct from eligibility for a **best-20-minute Performance result**. Both consume trusted underlying evidence, but they answer different questions. P4-01 accepts appropriately scoped, measured power training-stress intervals, FIT short-gap estimates and continuous ≥600-second observed partial segments; the separate Performance-v2 assessment requires an eligible 1,200-second best-20 window. The training model must not reject otherwise qualified power solely because a complete best-20 interval cannot be calculated. This clarifies existing P4-01 evidence intent, **not** a relaxation of its power source/gap/timer/FTP protections or authorization to change Performance-v2.

The **2026-10-07 virtual interval-workout regression** showed RideWorks using a 38.45-point Strava API HR summary while the Elevate reference used approximately 67 points of power-based stress. A subsequent private-source diagnosis found that the ride **passes** Performance-v2 eligibility and has **53.65 points of observed partial-power stress**; the current source priority selects HRSS over that subtotal, while incomplete API sampling prevents an independently verified whole-workout stress. Separately, requiring Performance-v2 best-20 eligibility hides 117 other P4-01-compliant virtual-ride power candidates. The earlier behavior tests selected high-load examples based on RideWorks' existing scores and therefore missed this case. The evidence supports independent power-stress eligibility and evaluation of a **labeled session estimate**; it does not establish Elevate's 67 as a required result.

**Blocking acceptance:** Investigate and implement a source-selection and estimated-session treatment consistent with the Elevate-first product direction, only after reviewing the approved evidence scope and recorded omissions. Re-run independently selected known structured workouts, ordinary sessions, and stress/HR counterexamples for source stress and Fitness/Fatigue response; use Elevate as the **expected default behavioral method**, with explained deviations, not as a replacement source of measured watts or fixed numerical target. See [JIT §12–13](../tasks/P4-02.md). Owner Gate 2 remains not accepted.

## 2. Training stress sources

- **Measured power:** For trustworthy power-meter virtual rides, prefer power-derived training stress with a **labeled session-level estimate** where reasonable coverage and activity duration support it, following Elevate's practical methodology. Use the approved dated [FTP history](../../data/athlete/strava_ftp_history.csv). Preserve unchanged P4-01 FIT-only interval gap screen (≤15 seconds each, ≤1% total), timer pause/restart rules, API observed-only measurements and provenance for **measured/corrected interval and partial claims**; a new session estimate must remain distinguishable from those. Virtual Ride classification alone never proves a watt source is trustworthy.
- **Strava outdoor estimated watts:** Do not use as the primary stress input; their rider-specific agreement with experienced effort has been poor. Retain as reference information where appropriate, not authoritative measured power.
- **HR fallback:** When usable measured power is absent or its samples/duration cannot support a sufficiently representative *estimate*, estimate load from eligible measured HR information and a supported duration. For v1 use the existing threshold-normalized, Elevate-inspired approximate HRSS calculation. No universal HRSS↔power-stress multiplier.
- **Stream versus summary:** A timestamped HR stream with suitable duration/coverage is preferred. Detailed-stream completeness requirements apply to *that stream's* reliability; they do not automatically invalidate a separate source-reported average HR and compatible same-source moving/active duration. Such a pairing can qualify for an **HR summary estimate** even if the detailed stream is defective, provided validity/compatibility checks pass. Preserve the failed-stream reason, duration basis, coverage uncertainty and approximate method label. A summary is not independently verified full-session coverage; pauses may be uncertain. Do not invent active time, fill unknown HR samples, assume CSV duration units, or silently choose among conflicting candidates.
- **Partials:** Keep the P4-01 observed partial-power subtotal and its missing-duration scope distinct from a proposed session-level power estimate. If a session estimate is not defensible, select suitable HR stress or a clearly incomplete partial subtotal under an explicitly justified rule. A partial-HR candidate is not approved as a default v1 replacement. Never add power and HR contributions for a single ride.

**Selected HR parameter assumptions (v1):** Resting HR **58 bpm**, approximate max HR **158 bpm**, modeled threshold **143 bpm** using the specified fallback. Apply as **retrospective modeling assumptions**, not asserted historical measurements, when accepted dated HR state is absent. Use accepted date-effective settings where genuinely available. Retain source/date/method version and recalculate when better settings become available. The JIT specifies the fixed 1.92 response coefficient; do not infer sex or treat the threshold as clinically measured.

**Unavailable stress:** Preserve `unavailable` in evidence and inspection, but use a numerical zero contribution to daily model stress for an unscored ride; dates with no recorded rides have zero *recorded* model contribution. Neither situation proves a rest day. This pragmatic curve convention is not permission to reject otherwise suitable approximate evidence. Scored coverage should be shown so substantial gaps are not confused with stable training.

## 3. Longitudinal calculations and presentation

- **Fitness:** 42-day exponential update of daily selected stress.
- **Fatigue:** 7-day exponential update of daily selected stress.
- **Form:** Beginning-of-day value based on **previous day's** Fitness minus Fatigue. This is an indicator, not an individualized readiness or overtraining diagnosis.
- **Initialization:** Zero before the earliest usable recorded stress, with the early modeled period identified as provisional. Do not extrapolate a current FTP into dates before its known effective history.
- **Not in v1:** Future training projections, fixed Form readiness/overload zones, prescriptions or extensive HR calibration.

The [approved Training State screen](P4-02-training-state-screen.md) contains a dominant interactive chart, independently selectable Fitness/Fatigue/Form series, synchronized daily stress strip, compact summary metrics, hover and keyboard/date inspection, persistent selected-day ride/source details, and separate 7-/42-day stress and observed-work (kJ) evidence. Home links to this view. Work in kJ is measured observed power, **not** a substitute for or conversion from HR stress.

## 4. Implementation evidence and regression examples

The first P4-02 implementation correctly applied the 42-/7-day arithmetic and preserved original data, but **overrestricted HR input selection**: at least 99% stream coverage, adjacent HR intervals ≤15 s and suppression of summary estimation whenever an invalid detailed stream was present. The resulting outdoor coverage was 7/148 scored rides despite additional source-reported HR information. These conditions are useful evidence screens for fully recorded streams, **not a general veto** on separately qualified HR-summary estimates.

The diagnostic identified three recent outdoor rides with potentially usable same-source HR/duration summaries. The **2026-09-16 Springwater Corridor Trail** ride is a mandatory *private* regression example: the initial model marked it unavailable (zero numeric contribution) while Elevate reported approximately 151 HRSS. The correction must derive an independent score from eligible RideWorks inputs and demonstrate the resulting daily/longitudinal response. Neither 151 nor the displayed Elevate graph is an imported model target. More historical outdoor activities still lack defensible duration/coverage and may remain unavailable; do not manufacture data to improve coverage statistics.

**Verification:** Source-selection cases (including defective stream + eligible summary), source/version provenance and unknown scope; impact on representative actual rides, daily trends and next-day Form; existing P4-01 power rules; browser chart inspection; source/original preservation. See the [P4-02 JIT](../tasks/P4-02.md) and [diagnostic report](../../reports/P4-02/review-followup.md).
